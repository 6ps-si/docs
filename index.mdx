## What 6PS Studio is

6PS Studio is a browser platform for hosting, viewing, and sharing 3D models and 360°
virtual walkthroughs, no separate software to install. Beyond hosting, the editors bring
AI directly into the workflow: restage furniture, preview a renovation, or edit a room or
model image right inside the browser, then publish the update immediately.
