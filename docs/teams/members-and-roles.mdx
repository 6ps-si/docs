The **Team members** list in the **Team** tab of your settings shows everyone in your team, their email, and their role. Owners and admins use it to invite people and manage them.

## Inviting someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add member">
    In **Team members**, click **Add member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Type the person's email under **Member email** and choose their **Role**: **Admin** or **Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Add**. The person is listed as **Pending** until they respond.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog notes that the person must already have an account on studio.6ps.si. After you add them, they receive an invite and must accept it themselves. Only owners and admins can invite.

A person can belong to one team at a time. If the person is already in another team, or has already been invited to yours, the invite is not sent. If your team has used all of its seats, the dialog says **Seat limit reached. The owner must purchase more seats.** See [Seats and shared quota](/docs/teams/seats-and-quota).

## Accepting and declining

An invited person sees a **Team invite** window when they sign in. It reads **You've been invited to join a team**, and shows the team name, the role, and who invited them. They click **Accept** to join or **Decline** to turn it down.

The same invites are listed under **Pending invites** in the **Team** tab. Accepting checks the team's seat limit again, so an invite can fail if the team filled up while it was waiting.

## Roles

The **Team members** list uses three roles.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can do, plus changing other members' roles and removing admins. The person who creates the team is the owner. |
| **Admin** | Invites members, revokes invites, removes members, edits and deletes team content, and manages the team logo. |
| **Member** | Sees the team's projects in **My Team**. |

Roles decide what someone can do with content that belongs to the team. In **My Team**, owners, admins, and editors can edit team content, and only owners and admins can delete it. See [Organizations](/docs/teams/organizations).

## Changing a role

Only the owner can change roles. In the **Role** column, click the role of an admin or member and pick **Admin** or **Member**, or open the **Options** menu and choose **Make admin** or **Make member**. You cannot change your own role, and the owner's role stays fixed.

## Removing someone

Open the **Options** menu on a member and choose **Remove from team**. Owners can remove admins and members. Admins can remove members. Nobody can remove the owner.

## Revoking an invite

An invite that has not been accepted stays in the list marked **Pending**. Owners and admins see these rows. Click the **x** on the row, labeled **Revoke invite**, to cancel it.
