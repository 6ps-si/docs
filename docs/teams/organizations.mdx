A team, also called an organization, gives a group of people one shared library, one pool of storage, and one pool of AI tokens. Members see each other's team projects in **My Team** in the library.

<Frame><img src="/images/teams/team-settings.webp" alt="The Team tab in user settings with the team logo, Team name and Description fields, the Team Usage panel, and the Team members list" /></Frame>

## Creating a team

In your account settings, open the **Team** tab, click **Create team**, and enter a **Team name**. You become the team's **Owner**. See [Members and roles](/docs/teams/members-and-roles) for what each role can do.

## Team details

The top of the **Team** tab holds the **Team name**, **Description**, and **Logo**. Click the logo to change it. The name and logo appear in the **Save as:** choice when you publish, and beside **My Team** in the library.

## Team usage

The **Team Usage** panel shows the team's plan, seats, shared storage, and AI tokens. See [Checking what is left](/docs/teams/seats-and-quota#checking-what-is-left).

## The shared library

The **My Team** section of your [library](/docs/library/overview) collects every project that belongs to the team, including private ones. Visibility still decides who outside the team can open a project. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership).

## Moving a project into the team

Pick your team's name in **Save as:** when you publish a project, or later in **Scene info**, to put it in **My Team**. See [Personal and team ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#personal-and-team-ownership).

## Sharing with individual people

The publish dialog shows a **Collaborate** button for sharing a project with teams or with a person by email. It appears in the dialog but is not yet available. To give people access today, add them to the team. See [Members and roles](/docs/teams/members-and-roles).
