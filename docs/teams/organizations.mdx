A team, also called an organization, gives a group of people one shared library, one pool of storage, and one pool of AI tokens. Members see each other's team projects in **My Team** in the library.

## Creating a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Open your account settings and select the **Team** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the team">
    If you are not in a team, the tab says **You're not part of any team**. Click **Create team**, enter a **Team name**, and click **Create team** again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You become the team's **Owner**. See [Members and roles](/docs/teams/members-and-roles) for what each role can do.

<Note>
  Teams are available on plans that include them. If yours does not, the **Team** tab shows a **Business** notice that reads **This is a higher tier feature**, with an **Upgrade now** button that opens the plan tab.
</Note>

A person can belong to one team at a time. If someone has invited you, the **Team** tab lists it under **Pending invites** with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons. If you have no invite yet, it says **No pending invites. Wait for a team admin to invite you.**

## Team details

The top of the **Team** tab holds the team's details:

- **Team name** is the name members see.
- **Description** is a short note about the team.
- **Logo** is the round picture at the left. Click it to open **Choose a team logo**, pick an image, and click **Save**. Owners and admins can click **Remove** under the logo to take it off.

Your team name and logo appear in the **Save as:** choice when you publish a project, and beside the **My Team** section title in the library. Click **Save changes** to store edits to the name and description.

## Team usage

The **Team Usage** panel shows the team's plan and the shared limits at a glance:

- The plan name, the number of seats in use, and the number of models.
- **Shared Storage**, used against the total, with a bar that changes color as it fills.
- **AI Tokens**, used against the monthly total, with the tokens **remaining**, any **extra bank**, and the date the allowance **Resets**.

See [Seats and shared quota](/docs/teams/seats-and-quota).

## The shared library

The **My Team** section of your [library](/docs/library/overview) collects every project that belongs to the team, whoever uploaded it. Use the **Shared** dropdown to filter by the member who created a model.

Members see each other's projects in **My Team**, including private ones. Visibility still decides who outside the team can open a project. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership).

## Moving a project into the team

Choose where a project lives with **Save as:** when you publish it, or later in **Scene info** when you edit it. Pick **Personal** to keep it in **My Scenes**, or pick your team's name to put it in **My Team**. Storage comes from the team's plan while you are a member, so the choice controls who can see and manage the project. See [Personal and team ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#personal-and-team-ownership).

## Sharing with individual people

The publish dialog shows a **Collaborate** button for sharing a project with teams or with a person by email. It appears in the dialog but is not yet available. To give people access today, add them to the team. See [Members and roles](/docs/teams/members-and-roles).
