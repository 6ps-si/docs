## Signing in

You can browse 6PS Studio and try the 3D Editor and Virtual Walkthrough without an account.
Publishing a project and managing your content requires signing in.

Select **Sign in** from the user menu in the top right of the navigation bar to start the sign-in
flow.

## Your profile

Open your avatar in the top right, then select **Settings** to reach your profile.

Under **General**, you can update:

- Your profile photo. Select the photo to open the upload dialog, choose an image, and save. Photos
  are cropped to a square and must be under 5 MB.
- Username, first name, last name, address, and phone number.

Under **Security**, you can change your account email or password. Both open in a new tab.

## Language

6PS Studio is available in English and Slovenian. Switch languages from the **Language** option
in the avatar dropdown menu, or from the language toggle in your profile settings. The switcher is
visible only when you're signed in.
