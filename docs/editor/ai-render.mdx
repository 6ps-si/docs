**AI Render** captures the view you are looking at in the 3D Editor and turns it into a rendered image based on your description. It is built to cut the wait for a visual from about an hour of conventional rendering to roughly ten seconds, so you can try ideas quickly and share them.

Treat the result as a fast way to explore and communicate a design. It does not replace a final render.

## Before you start

- Load a model and frame the view you want to render. AI Render uses the view exactly as it appears on screen.
- Each render uses AI tokens. The panel shows the cost per use and your remaining balance before you run anything.

## Render an image

Open **AI Render** in the right sidebar, or the **AI** tab on a phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an aspect ratio">
    Pick **16:9**, **4:3**, **1:1**, **3:4**, or **9:16**. A frame appears in the viewer to show what will be captured. Nothing is selected by default, and you need to choose one to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    Write your request in the **Prompt** box, which starts with **Describe the edit…**. Say what should change in the captured view, such as materials, lighting, or furnishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add references (optional)">
    Add a reference image and up to four colors, as described below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Render">
    Click **Capture & Render**. The button stays disabled until you have both a prompt and an aspect ratio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The result opens over the viewer. Use **Download** to save it, or close it to return to the editor. Click **New render** to start again.

## Reference images

A reference image guides the AI with a second picture. Under **Reference image** you have two options:

- **Upload image** lets you pick a JPEG, PNG, or WebP file.
- **Use last render** appears after your first render and reuses the last render from this session. This helps you refine a result step by step.

Click the small **x** on the preview to remove the reference.

## Exact colors

**Color references** let you name the exact colors the render should use, which is more reliable than describing a color in words.

1. Click the **+** button. You can add up to four colors.
2. In the text box, say what the color is for, for example "walls".
3. Click the color swatch and choose the color.

Use the **x** next to a color to remove it.

## Upscale a render

After your first render, the **Upscale** mode becomes available in the **Mode** selector. Before then it shows **Run a render first**.

Upscale enlarges and sharpens your most recent render. It needs no prompt. Select **Upscale**, then click **Upscale Render**. Click **New upscale** afterwards to run it again.

## What it costs

The panel shows **AI tokens**, the cost of a render or upscale as **per usage**, and how many tokens you have **left**. Your balance also appears on the stats bar in your [library](/docs/library/overview#your-usage-at-a-glance).

<Note>
  AI output is watermarked on the free plan. The watermark is applied when the image is shown, so it disappears from your existing results as soon as you move to a plan without one.
</Note>
