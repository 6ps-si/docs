## What it does

Captures the view you are looking at and renders an image from it. Position it as cutting
rendering time from about an hour to roughly ten seconds. Do not describe the result as a
finished deliverable.

## Render or upscale

The two modes and when to use each.

## Describing what you want

Writing the prompt, and choosing an aspect ratio.

## Reference images

Uploading a reference, and reusing your last render as the reference for the next one.

## Exact colors

Picking up to four colors the render should use.

## What it costs

Each render uses AI tokens. The panel shows the cost and your remaining balance before you
run it.

## On the free plan

Results carry a watermark. Watermarking is applied when the image is fetched, so it
disappears from existing results as soon as you move to a paid plan.
