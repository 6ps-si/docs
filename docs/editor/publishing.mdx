Publishing uploads your scene and gives it a link. Everything you set up in the editor goes live together: the models, camera positions, buttons, and scene settings.

## Before you publish

You need:

- **A signed-in account.** You can build in the editor without one, and Studio asks you to sign in when you click **Publish**.
- **An active plan.** Without one, the publish dialog tells you that you need an active subscription and offers a **Plan** button.
- **At least one model** in the scene.
- **A name** for the scene.

## Naming your project

In the **Scene info** panel, fill in:

- **Name** is the name shown to visitors when they open the model.
- **Description** is shown to visitors alongside the viewer. Use it to explain what they are looking at.

Publishing is blocked until the **Name** field has a value. If it is empty, the dialog says **Enter a scene name in the Scene Info panel before publishing.**

## Choosing visibility

The **Visibility** selector in the same panel decides who can open the model. Set it before you publish. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership) for what each option does.

## Publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Click **Publish** at the top of **Scene info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog asks **Are you sure you want to publish?** and shows a thumbnail preview. The thumbnail is a picture of your first camera position, so put the view you want to represent the model first. See [Camera positions](/docs/editor/camera-positions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where it lives">
    If you belong to a team, use **Save as:** to choose **Personal** or your team's name. Storage comes from your organization's plan while you are a member, so this choice controls who can see and manage the project. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#personal-and-team-ownership).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Click **Continue**. A progress bar shows the upload, and it moves to the share options when the model is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If publishing fails, the dialog returns to the confirmation step and shows the reason so you can try again.

## Share options

When your model is live, **Export options** shows:

- **View model online** opens the published model in a new tab.
- A **QR code**, with a download button, and the **Share Link** with a copy button.
- **Embed as iFrame** copies an iframe snippet to paste into your website. This option is available on the Pro plan.

**Collaborate** and **Code Embed** appear in the dialog but are not yet available. See [Links and embedding](/docs/sharing/links-and-embedding) for more on sharing.

## Updating a published model

To change a published model, open it from your [library](/docs/library/overview). The **Publish** button becomes **Save**. It saves your changes to the same link, so existing links, QR codes, and embeds keep working. The button shows **Saving…** and then **Saved**.

You can change camera positions, buttons, scene settings, the name and description, visibility, and ownership. You cannot add or delete models in a published scene. To use different model files, publish a new model.
