Publishing uploads your scene and gives it a link. Everything you set up in the editor goes live together: the models, camera positions, buttons, and scene settings.

## Before you publish

- **Sign in.** You can build in the editor without an account, and Studio asks you to sign in when you click **Publish**.
- **Have an active plan.** Without one, the publish dialog offers a **Plan** button.
- **Add at least one model.**
- **Fill in Scene info.** Enter a **Name**, which publishing requires, and optionally a **Description**. Visitors see both. Set **Visibility** to decide who can open the model. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership).

## Publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Click **Publish** at the top of **Scene info**. The dialog shows a thumbnail taken from your first camera position, so put your best view first. See [Camera positions](/docs/editor/camera-positions#ordering).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where it lives">
    If you belong to a team, use **Save as:** to choose **Personal** or your team's name. See [Personal and team ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#personal-and-team-ownership).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Click **Continue**. When the upload finishes, the share options appear. If publishing fails, the dialog shows the reason so you can try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share options

When your model is live, **Export options** shows:

- **View model online**
- A **QR code** and the **Share Link**
- **Embed as iFrame**, available on the Pro plan

**Collaborate** appears in the dialog but is not yet available. See [Share links and embedding](/docs/sharing/links-and-embedding).

## Updating a published model

To change a published model, open it from your [library](/docs/library/overview). The **Publish** button becomes **Save**. It saves your changes to the same link, so existing links, QR codes, and embeds keep working.

You can change camera positions, buttons, scene settings, the name and description, visibility, and ownership. You cannot add or delete models in a published scene. To use different model files, publish a new model.
