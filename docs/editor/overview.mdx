In the 3D Editor you load a model, decide how it looks and behaves for viewers, and publish it as a link or an embed.

The editor handles standard mesh models and 3D Gaussian Splatting files. That covers large building scenes as well as single-object scans for product pages. See [Uploading models](/docs/editor/uploading-models) for the full list.

Viewers explore a published model live in their browser. The aim is to shorten the wait for something you can show a client from about an hour of rendering to roughly ten seconds.

## The layout

- **Viewer**, in the middle. Before you add a model, it is a drop zone that says **Drag and drop your 3D asset here**.
- **Left sidebar**:
  - **Objects** lists the models in your scene. See [Uploading models](/docs/editor/uploading-models) and [Model settings](/docs/editor/model-settings).
  - **Positions** holds the saved camera views. See [Camera positions](/docs/editor/camera-positions).
  - **Buttons** places clickable points in the scene. See [Hotspots and buttons](/docs/editor/hotspots).
  - **Advanced** holds visual effects. See [Scene and environment](/docs/editor/scene-and-environment#advanced-display-settings).
- **Right sidebar**:
  - **Scene info** holds the name, description, visibility, and the **Publish** button. See [Publishing a model](/docs/editor/publishing).
  - Settings for the selected position, button, and model, and **Scene Settings** for the background and lighting. See [Scene and environment](/docs/editor/scene-and-environment).
  - **AI Render** creates an AI-edited image from the current view. See [AI render](/docs/editor/ai-render).
  - **Statistics** jumps to the view figures for the model. See [Engagement and analytics](/docs/sharing/engagement-and-analytics).
