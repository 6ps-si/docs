The **Scene Settings** panel controls what surrounds your model: the background color, an optional 360° or HDR environment image, the camera's field of view, and the logo overlay. The **Advanced** panel adds visual effects. Both apply to the whole scene, and to every model in it.

## Background color

Open **Scene Settings** in the right sidebar. While no environment image is set, a color picker labeled **Background Color** appears. Choose a color, and the viewer background changes immediately. The color also carries a transparency value.

## Environment images

Click the **HDRI** button, or read the hint **Add a 360° image or HDR environment** above it, to open the **Select HDRI** dialog. Drag a file into the dialog, or click it to browse, then click **Add background**.

The dialog accepts `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.hdr`, and `.exr` files. Studio detects the type from the file extension and shows it before you confirm:

| Type | Files | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **360° Image** | `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` | An equirectangular photo shown as a background dome around the scene. |
| **HDR Environment** | `.hdr`, `.exr` | A prefiltered map that lights the scene. |

Once an environment image is set, the **Background Color** picker is replaced by the controls below. To remove the image, click the red trash button next to **HDRI**, and the color picker returns.

## Adjusting the environment

The controls depend on the type of image.

| Control | Applies to | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rotation** | Both | Turns the background. A 360° image has **X**, **Y**, and **Z** fields. An HDR environment has a **Y** field only. Values run from -360 to 360. |
| **Scale** | 360° image | The size of the background sphere. |
| **Intensity** | HDR environment | How strongly the environment lights the scene. |

Rotate the environment to line up the part of the image you want behind the model, or to change where the light comes from.

## Field of view

**Field of view** sets the camera's default view angle in degrees, from 10 to 170. It controls how zoomed in the view looks. It applies to the whole scene, including every camera position.

## 6PS Logo

The **6PS Logo** switch shows or hides the 6PS logo overlay in the viewer.

## Advanced display settings

In the left sidebar, open **Advanced**, then **Post Processing**. Each switch turns on one visual effect:

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sharpen** | Sharpens the image slightly to counteract softness. |
| **FXAA Anti-aliasing** | Smooths jagged edges. |
| **Bloom** | Adds a soft glow around bright areas. Marked as expensive. |
| **Depth of Field** | Blurs objects outside the camera's focus distance. Marked as very expensive. |
| **Chromatic Aberration** | Adds a subtle color fringe at the edges of the frame. |
| **Vignette** | Darkens the corners of the view. |
| **Color Grading** | Applies a color-grading look to the whole scene. |

Effects marked expensive or very expensive ask more of the viewer's device, so test them on a phone before you publish.
