Buttons add interactive points to your model. A visitor clicks one to read a note, or to open a linked page. You manage them in the **Buttons** panel on the left and edit them in **Button Settings** on the right.

## Two kinds of buttons

The **Buttons** panel lists both kinds, each with its icon and name.

| Kind | How it behaves | Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-screen button | Stays in the same place on the screen while the camera moves. | An X and Y offset from the center of the view. |
| 3D button | Sits on a point of the model and moves with it as the camera moves. | X, Y, and Z coordinates in the scene. |

Use an on-screen button for something that should always be visible, such as a note about the whole scene or a link to your website. Use a 3D button to label a specific part of the model.

## Adding an on-screen button

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the button">
    Click the **+** button in the **Buttons** panel. A new button named **Canvas button** followed by a number appears in the list and in the viewer, and its settings open on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Drag the button to where you want it in the viewer. You can also type its **X** and **Y** values under **Button Location**. Both values run from -50 to 50, measured from the center of the view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what it does">
    Choose a **Button Type** and fill in its content, as described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

3D buttons appear in the same list when a scene already contains them. Select one to edit it. Its **Button Location** has **X**, **Y**, and **Z** fields for the position in the scene.

## Button types

The **Button Type** setting decides what happens when a visitor clicks the button.

| Type | What the visitor sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Icon with Text** | A popup with the content you write in **Button Description**. |
| **Frame** | The page at your **Link** URL, opened in a frame on top of the scene. |

For a **Frame** button, turn on **Fullscreen** to open the linked content over the whole screen in place of a popup frame.

## Writing the content

For an **Icon with Text** button, write in the **Button Description** editor. Use the expand icon on the editor to work in a larger window. The toolbar offers:

- Bold, italic, and underline
- Heading 2 and Heading 3
- Bullet and numbered lists
- Left, center, and right alignment
- Links
- A horizontal rule
- Text color, from a set of preset colors

<Note>
  The **Button Name** field is an internal label. It appears in the editor's button list and is not shown to visitors.
</Note>

## Appearance

- **Button Icon** shows a grid of icons. Click one to use it. You can also click the icon next to a button in the list to change it.
- **Size** sets how large an on-screen button appears. A 3D button has three size fields: **Min**, **Size**, and **Max**.

## Editing and removing

- Click a button in the list, or click it in the viewer, to select it and open **Button Settings**.
- Click outside the panel to deselect it.
- Hover over a button in the list, open its menu, and choose **Delete** to remove it.
