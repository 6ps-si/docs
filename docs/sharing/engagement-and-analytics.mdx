## Viewing statistics

Statistics appear in two places:

- **In the editor**, below the workspace. **Statistics** in the right sidebar jumps there.
- **On the project's viewer page**, at the bottom.

Total views across all your projects are on the [library stats bar](/docs/library/overview#your-usage-at-a-glance).

<Note>
  Your own visits, and visits by members of your organization, are not counted, so the numbers reflect your audience.
</Note>

## What is measured

**Models** show these cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Average view time** | How long the viewer stays open on average, in seconds. |
| **Average load time** | How long the model takes to finish loading and rendering, in seconds. |
| **Views** | The number of times the viewer was opened, as a total and as a bar chart. |
| **Size** | The size of the published scene, shown against a mobile-friendly benchmark. |

**Views** can be shown by **Week**, **Month**, or **Year**. On the **Size** card, green means good for mobile, yellow may cause performance issues, and red is not recommended.

**Walkthroughs** show **Average view time**, **Views**, and the number of **rooms**.

<Tip>
  A slow average load time or a large size can lose visitors on phones. If your numbers look high, publish lighter models or fewer high-resolution rooms.
</Tip>

## Comments

Every published project has a **Comments** section on its viewer page. Visitors must be signed in to comment, and comments can be up to 2000 characters. You can edit and delete your own comments, and the project owner can delete any comment on it. Only people who can open the project can read its comments.

## Likes

Signed-in visitors can like a project with the heart on its viewer page. Likes feed the **Popularity** sort in the [library](/docs/library/overview#sections).

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar tells you when an AI job completes or fails, with tokens refunded on failure, and when your subscription expires in less than 3 days.
