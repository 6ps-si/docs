## Sharing a link

When publishing finishes, the share dialog shows the **Share Link** and a downloadable QR code. Later, use **SHARE** on the project's viewer page.

The viewer page shows the live viewer, the project name, creator, and description, likes, comments, and statistics. On a phone, visitors tap the viewer to start interacting. Who can open the link depends on the project's [visibility](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership).

As the owner, you also see **USE IN WEBSITE** and **EDIT** on the viewer page.

## Embedding in a site

Copy the embed code with **Embed as iFrame** in the share dialog, or with **USE IN WEBSITE** on the viewer page, and paste it into your site's HTML. It looks like this:

```html
<iframe src="https://studio.6ps.si/view/?id=YOUR_PROJECT_ID" width="100%" height="600" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>
```

Change `width` and `height` to fit your page. In WordPress, paste the code into a **Custom HTML** block. If you use the iframe plugin, **USE IN WEBSITE** also offers a shortcode.

## Restricting where it can be embedded

To make a project load only on your own website, use [domain locking](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#domain-locking).
