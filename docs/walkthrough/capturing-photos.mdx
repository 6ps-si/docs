## 360 cameras

Photos from a 360 camera such as Insta360 can be imported directly.

## Image requirements

Equirectangular format and the resolutions that work well. Take the numbers from the image
processor notes rather than estimating.

## Flat photos

When a normal photo is a better fit than a 360 one.

## Shooting tips

Practical advice: tripod placement, consistent lighting, one shot per room, keeping the
camera at eye height.

## Removing the tripod

The AI room editing tool can paint out a tripod or other unwanted objects. Link forward to
it.
