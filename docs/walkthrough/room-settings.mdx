## Naming rooms

Room names and where visitors see them.

## The opening view

Setting the direction a room opens at, either by capturing the current view or by entering
angles and field of view.

## Camera behavior

Rotation speed, inverting rotation, and zoom, set for the whole scene.

## Colour adjustments

Brightness, contrast, saturation, tint, and tint strength, with a reset.

## Per-room overrides

A single room can override the scene's colour settings, which helps when one photo is darker
than the rest.
