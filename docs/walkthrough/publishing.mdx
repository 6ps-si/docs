## Before you publish

- Add at least one image.
- In **Scene info**, enter a **Name**, which publishing requires, and optionally a **Description**. Visitors see both. Set **Visibility** to decide who can open the tour. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership).

## Publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Click **Publish** at the top of **Scene info**. If you are signed out, a sign-in window opens first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    If you belong to a team, choose **Personal** or your team's name under **Save as:**. See [Personal and team ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#personal-and-team-ownership). Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for every room">
    The panel shows each room's stage: **Compressing**, **Processing 360°** for 360° photos, **Uploading**, and **Done**. The work happens in your browser, so keep the tab open until it finishes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Click **Done** to open the share options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A room that fails shows **Error** with its message. Click **Retry** to start again. If one photo keeps failing, [replace its image](/docs/walkthrough/building-a-tour#replacing-and-deleting) and publish again.

## Share options

**Publish options** opens when the walkthrough is live:

- **View the walkthrough**
- A **QR code** and the **Share Link**
- **Export iframe**, available on the Pro plan

See [Share links and embedding](/docs/sharing/links-and-embedding) for more on sharing.

## Editing a published tour

Open the walkthrough from your [library](/docs/library/overview). The **Publish** button becomes **Save**, and it keeps the same link.

Saving uploads only the rooms you replaced, added, or removed, with the same progress panel.

Adding or deleting whole scenes is not available on a published walkthrough. You can still adjust rooms, buttons, and settings. To change the set of scenes, publish a new walkthrough.
