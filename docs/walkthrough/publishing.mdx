Publishing prepares every room photo, uploads it, and gives the walkthrough a link you can share. This page explains what to set first, what the progress panel shows, and how to change a tour after it is live.

## Before you publish

Set these in the **Scene info** panel in the right sidebar:

- **Name.** Publishing is blocked until the walkthrough has a name. If it is empty, the dialog says **Give your tour a name before publishing.**
- **Description.** Shown to visitors alongside the tour.
- **Visibility.** Decides who can open the tour. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership).

You also need at least one image in the tour. Without one, the dialog says **Add at least one image before publishing.**

If you belong to a team, the confirmation dialog has a **Save as:** choice of **Personal** or your team's name. See [Visibility and ownership](/docs/library/visibility-and-ownership#personal-and-team-ownership).

## Publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Click **Publish** at the top of **Scene info**. If you are signed out, a sign-in window opens first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Publishing Tour** dialog explains that your tour is ready to process and publish, and that this processes all images and prepares them for upload. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the progress">
    The panel lists every room with its current stage, and an **Overall progress** bar sits below them. See the next section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    When every room is done, click **Done** to open the share options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What publishing does

Each room photo is prepared in your browser first, then uploaded. The stage label next to each room tells you where it is:

| Stage | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | The room is in the queue. |
| **Compressing** | The image is being reduced to a size that loads well. |
| **Processing 360°** | A 360° photo is being converted into the form the viewer uses. Flat rooms skip this stage. |
| **Uploading** | The prepared files are being sent to Studio. |
| **Done** | The room is finished. |
| **Error** | The room could not be published. |

Rooms are prepared two at a time, so a tour with many rooms takes longer than one with a few. The work happens in your browser, so keep the tab open until the panel shows **Done**. You cannot close the panel while it is running.

## If something fails

A room that fails is marked **Error** in the panel, and the message for that room appears next to it. Click **Retry** to go back to the confirmation step and start again. If a single photo keeps failing, replace that room's image and publish again. See [Building a tour](/docs/walkthrough/building-a-tour#replacing-a-room-image).

## Share options

**Publish options** opens when the walkthrough is live:

- **View the walkthrough** opens it in a new tab.
- A **QR code** with a download button.
- The **Share Link** with a copy button.
- **Export iframe** copies an embed snippet for your website. It is available on the Pro plan.

**Code Embed** appears in the dialog but is not yet available. See [Links and embedding](/docs/sharing/links-and-embedding) for more on sharing.

## Editing a published tour

Open the walkthrough from your [library](/docs/library/overview). The **Publish** button becomes **Save**, and it keeps the same link.

When you save, Studio uploads the rooms you replaced, added to a scene, or removed, and shows the same progress panel under the title **Saving Changes**. Removed rooms show a **Deleting** stage. Visibility and ownership changes are saved at the same time.

Adding or deleting whole scenes is not available on a published walkthrough. See [Building a tour](/docs/walkthrough/building-a-tour).
