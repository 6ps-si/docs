## What it does

Edit the room photo inside the walkthrough editor and publish the result, with no separate
photo software and no editing experience needed.

## Choosing an area

Paint a mask over the part of the room you want changed. Brush size, undo, clear, and done.

## Describing the change

Writing a prompt. Give worked examples: furnishing an empty room, previewing a renovation,
removing a tripod.

## Reviewing the result

Comparing before and after.

## Applying or reverting

Applying splices the edit back at full resolution. Reverting discards it.

## What it costs

The quota bar shows your AI token balance, the cost of one edit, and your usage.

## On the free plan

Results are watermarked until you move to a paid plan.
