title: "AI room editing"
description: "Stage furniture, preview a renovation, or remove objects directly in a 360 room."
---

**AI Editing** changes part of a room photo from inside the walkthrough editor. You paint over an area, describe the change, and Studio regenerates only that area. Use it to furnish an empty room, preview a renovation, or remove an object such as a tripod. It works on 360° and flat rooms.

<Frame><img src="/images/walkthrough/ai-room-editing.webp" alt="The walkthrough editor with a painted area over a room's floor and the AI Editing panel showing the brush size slider and Done, Undo, and Clear buttons" /></Frame>

## Before you start

- Select a room with an image. The **AI Editing** panel in the right sidebar, or the **AI** tab on a phone, stays dimmed until you do.
- Each edit uses AI tokens. The bar at the bottom of the panel shows the cost **per usage** and the tokens you have **left**.

## Make an edit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the area">
    Click **Select Area** and paint over the part of the room you want to change. Adjust **Brush size** as needed, then click **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    In the **Prompt** box, describe the change for the painted area only, then click **Generate**. For example: "a modern grey sofa and a wooden coffee table", "light oak flooring", or "remove the tripod".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    <div className="side-image">
      <Frame><img src="/images/walkthrough/ai-room-editing-review.webp" alt="The AI Editing panel showing Before and After images side by side with Retry and Apply buttons" /></Frame>
    </div>

    The panel shows **Before** and **After** side by side. To try again, change the prompt and click **Retry**. The painted area stays selected.

    <div className="clear-float" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply**. Studio blends the result into the room photo at full resolution. **Revert last edit** undoes the last apply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Edits are saved when you save or publish the walkthrough. Reloading the editor before that discards them. For a 360° room loaded from an existing walkthrough, **Revert last edit** does not change what you see within the session.
</Note>

<Note>
  AI output is watermarked on the free plan. The watermark is applied when the image is shown, so it disappears from your results when you move to a plan without one.
</Note>
