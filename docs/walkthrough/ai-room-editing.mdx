**AI Editing** changes part of a room photo from inside the walkthrough editor. You paint over the area you want to change, describe the change, and Studio produces a new version of that area. You can furnish an empty room, preview a renovation, or remove an unwanted object such as a camera tripod. There is no separate photo-editing software to learn.

It works on 360° rooms and on flat rooms.

## Before you start

Select a room in the **Scenes** list. The **AI Editing** panel in the right sidebar, or the **AI** tab on a phone, is dimmed until a room with an image is selected.

Each edit uses AI tokens. The panel shows **AI credits**, the cost of one edit as **per usage**, and how many tokens are **left**.

## Choosing an area

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Editing">
    Open the panel and click **Select Area**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paint the area">
    Paint over the part of the room you want to change with the mouse or your finger. A banner reads **Paint over the area you want to edit**. Use the **Brush size** slider to change the brush width. A smaller brush gives cleaner edges, and a larger brush covers more per stroke.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust if needed">
    Click **Undo** to remove your last stroke, or **Clear** to start over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Done**. The panel shows a preview of the selected area with your paint on it, and the message **Area selected ✓**. Click **Clear** next to that message to discard the selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Studio works on the painted area rather than the whole panorama, so the rest of the room stays untouched.

## Describing the change

Write what you want in the **Prompt** box, then click **Generate**. The button stays disabled until you have written a prompt. Describe the change for the painted area only. For example:

- Furnish an empty room: "a modern grey sofa and a wooden coffee table, minimalist style."
- Preview a renovation: "light oak flooring and white walls."
- Remove an object: "remove the tripod."

The box's placeholder suggests a short style of request: **Describe your edit, e.g. "remove the chair"**.

While the edit runs, the panel shows **Generating…**. Click **Cancel** to stop and go back to the selected area.

## Reviewing the result

When the edit finishes, the panel shows **Before** and **After** images of the painted area side by side. If the result is not what you wanted, change the **Prompt** and click **Retry**. The area you painted stays selected, so you do not need to paint it again. Click the **x** to discard the result.

## Applying or reverting

Click **Apply** when you are happy. Studio blends the new pixels into the room photo at its full resolution, and the room in the viewer updates. You can then click **Select Area** to make another edit.

Click **Revert last edit** to go back to the image before your last apply.

<Note>
  Your edits stay in the editor until you save or publish the walkthrough. For a 360° room that was loaded from an existing walkthrough, **Revert last edit** does not change what you see within the session. Nothing has been saved, so reloading the editor without saving discards the edit.
</Note>

Publish the walkthrough to make the edited rooms live. See [Publishing a walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/publishing).

## What it costs

The bar at the bottom of the panel reads **AI credits** with the cost per edit and the tokens you have left, and a progress bar shows how much of your allowance is used. Retrying an edit runs it again. Your balance also appears on the stats bar in your [library](/docs/library/overview#your-usage-at-a-glance).

## On the free plan

AI output is watermarked on the free plan. The watermark is applied when the image is shown, so it disappears from your results when you move to a plan without one.
