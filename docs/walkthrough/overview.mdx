The Virtual Walkthrough editor turns your room photos into a tour, links the rooms with buttons, and publishes it as a link or an embed. It runs in your browser, on a phone as well as on a desktop.

<Frame><img src="/images/walkthrough/overview.webp" alt="The Virtual Walkthrough editor with the Scenes list on the left, a 360 room in the viewer, and Scene info on the right" /></Frame>

## Scenes and rooms

- A **room** is one image, either a 360° panorama or a flat photo.
- A **scene** groups rooms, such as the rooms of one apartment. Use more than one scene to keep separate spaces apart, for example a building overview and each apartment.

## The layout

On a desktop the editor has three areas.

- **Left sidebar.** The **Scenes** list and the **Buttons** panel.
- **Viewer.** The live view of the selected room. Before you add anything, it is a drop zone that says **Drag & drop a 360° or flat image**.
- **Right sidebar.** **Scene info** with the name, description, visibility, and **Publish** button, followed by the settings for the walkthrough and the selected room.

## On a phone

<div className="side-image">
  <Frame><img src="/images/walkthrough/mobile.webp" alt="The walkthrough editor on a phone screen with a 360 room above and the bottom sheet open, showing the Info, Scenes, Buttons, Settings, and AI tabs" /></Frame>
</div>

The whole flow works on a phone. The panels move into a bottom sheet with tabs: **Info**, **Scenes**, **Buttons**, **Settings**, and **AI**. The **Buttons** and **Settings** tabs appear when the selected scene supports them. Tap a tab to open the sheet, and tap the handle or the viewer to close it.


This suits shooting on site and publishing before you leave. You can capture 360° photos of a property, then assemble and publish the tour from your phone on the way home.
