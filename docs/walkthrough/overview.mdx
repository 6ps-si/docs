A virtual walkthrough is a tour that visitors move through room by room, in their browser. You build it from photos, link the rooms together with buttons, and publish it as a link or an embed. The editor runs in your browser, on a phone as well as on a desktop.

## What a walkthrough is

A walkthrough is a set of connected rooms. A room can be a 360° panorama that a visitor looks around in, or a flat photo shown as a regular image. Buttons in a room take the visitor to another room or scene, show information, or open a link.

## Scenes and rooms

The editor organizes a walkthrough into scenes and rooms.

- A **scene** is a container. It is either a **360° Image** scene, a **Flat Image** scene, or an empty **Scene** that you fill in later.
- A **room** is one image inside a scene. Each room shows a **360°** or **FLAT** badge with its name.

One scene can hold several rooms, such as the living room, kitchen, and bedrooms of an apartment. Use more than one scene to keep separate spaces apart. For example, an overview photo of a building can be one scene and each apartment tour another, with buttons that link them.

## The layout

On a desktop the editor has three areas.

- **Left sidebar.** The **Scenes** list, with an **Add** menu, and the **Buttons** panel. Open a scene to see its rooms as cards with a thumbnail, badge, and name. On a room card you can replace the image or delete the room.
- **Viewer.** The live view of the selected room. Before you add anything, it is a drop zone that says **Drag & drop a 360° or flat image**.
- **Right sidebar.** **Scene info** with the name, description, visibility, and **Publish** button, followed by the settings for the walkthrough and the selected room.

## On a phone

The whole flow works on a phone. The panels move into a bottom sheet with tabs: **Info**, **Scenes**, **Buttons**, **Settings**, and **AI**. The **Buttons** and **Settings** tabs appear when the selected scene supports them. Tap a tab to open the sheet, and tap the handle or the viewer to close it.

This suits shooting on site and publishing before you leave. You can capture 360° photos of a property, then assemble and publish the tour from your phone on the way home.

## Trying it without an account

You can open the editor and try its features without signing in. You need an account to publish. When you click **Publish** while signed out, a sign-in window opens. See [Publishing a walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/publishing).
