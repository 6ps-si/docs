## What a walkthrough is

A set of connected rooms a visitor can move through. Rooms can be 360 panoramas or flat
photos.

## Scenes and rooms

How the two relate, and why you would use more than one scene.

## The layout

Room list on the left, live viewer in the middle, settings on the right, with a bottom sheet
on mobile.

## On a phone

The full build-and-publish flow works on a phone, which suits shooting on site and
publishing before you leave.
