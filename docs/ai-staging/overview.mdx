AI Virtual Staging turns a photo of a room into a staged or edited version of it. Upload an image, describe what you want, and Studio returns a new image you can download. It works on regular photos and on 360° panoramas.

Use it to add furniture to an empty room, preview a renovation, remove an object, or enlarge an image.

## Before you start

- You need a JPG, PNG, or WebP image. Each file can be up to 30 MB.
- You can open the page and load an image without signing in. Generating a result requires you to sign in.
- Each generation uses AI tokens. The cost appears under the generate button, and your remaining balance appears at the top of the page.

## Open AI Virtual Staging

From your [library](/docs/library/overview), click the dashed plus card in **My Scenes**. In the **Choose Project Type** dialog, pick **AI Virtual Staging** and click **Continue**.

## Edit an image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your image">
    Drag an image onto the **Original Image** panel, or click **Browse** and pick a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Edit mode">
    Make sure **Edit** is selected in the mode toggle. It is the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    Write your request in **Describe your staging requirements**. Name the furniture, where it goes, and the style. For example: "Add a modern grey sofa on the left side, wooden coffee table in center, minimalist style."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate Edit**. The result appears in the **Staged Result** panel when it is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A description is required in Edit mode. The button stays disabled until you enter one.

### Edit one area of a photo

On a regular photo, click **Select area to edit** and draw a rectangle over the part of the image you want to change, then click **Done**. The button changes to **Mask drawn - click to redraw** so you can adjust the rectangle. Your description then applies to that area.

### Panoramas

Studio checks the shape of your image. A 360° panorama, which has an aspect ratio of about 2:1, is recognized automatically and you see **Smart Detection: Recognized as a 360° Panorama.** Studio then corrects the seam where the panorama wraps around, so the edge of the image lines up in the viewer.

For a panorama you also get a **Renovate** checkbox. Turn it on to have the surfaces of the room, such as floor, walls, and ceiling, renovated as well as furnished. Describe both the surfaces and the furnishing in your description.

If Studio treats a regular image as a panorama, click **Switch to standard** to edit it as a normal photo.

## Upscale an image

Select **Upscale** in the mode toggle, add your image, and click **Upscale Image**. Upscale mode needs no description.

## Review and download your result

The **Staged Result** panel offers these controls:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Expand | Shows the image at full size. |
| 360° | Opens a panorama in an interactive viewer. Available for panoramas. |
| Download | Saves the image to your device. You can also click **Export Image**. |
| Refresh | Clears the current image and settings so you can start again. |

On a phone, the page is split into three screens. Swipe up or tap **View result** to move from your upload to the result, and **View history** to reach earlier generations. **Redo** on the result screen takes you back to the first screen.

<Note>
  AI output is watermarked on the free plan. The watermark is applied when the image is shown, so it disappears from your results when you move to a plan without one.
</Note>

## Previous generations

Sign in to see your history. Click **Previous Generations** at the bottom of the page on desktop, or use **View history** on a phone, to open it. Each entry shows whether it was **Edited** or **Upscaled**, when it was created, and its status. Click **See** to load a finished image back into the upload area, or use the download icon to save it. **Refresh** reloads the list.

<Warning>
  Generated images are stored for 7 days. Download anything you want to keep.
</Warning>

## While you wait

A generation takes a little while. You can leave the page. When it finishes, it appears in your history, and the bell in the top bar tells you. If a generation fails, the bell says so and the AI tokens are refunded. See [Engagement and analytics](/docs/sharing/engagement-and-analytics#notifications).
