## Sections

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **My Scenes** | The 3D models and walkthroughs you created yourself. |
| **My Team** | Everything that belongs to your organization, whoever uploaded it. |
| **Public** | Example models and walkthroughs shared by the community. |

You can browse Public without signing in. Sign in to see your own scenes and your team's content.

Each section has a **Search** box, an **All**, **Models**, and **Walkthroughs** filter, and a **Sort by** menu. Newest projects come first by default. **Popularity** sorts by likes. In My Team, the **Shared** dropdown shows only the models one member uploaded.

## Your usage at a glance

When you are signed in, the stats bar at the top of the page shows:

- **Scenes**: the total number of models and walkthroughs across My Scenes and My Team.
- **Views**: total views across your content. Hover over it to see the split between **Models** and **Walkthroughs**.
- **Storage**: the space you have used out of the space your plan allows, in GB.
- **AI tokens**: the AI tokens you have left.

## Starting a new project

Click the dashed plus card at the end of My Scenes to open the **Choose Project Type** dialog:

- **3D Editor** for 3D models. See [3D Editor](/docs/editor/overview).
- **Virtual Walkthrough** for 360° tours. See [Virtual Walkthrough](/docs/walkthrough/overview).
- **AI Virtual Staging** for furnishing, renovating, editing, or upscaling a single image. See [AI Virtual Staging](/docs/ai-staging/overview).

## Locked sections

My Team is locked when you are signed out, or when you are not in an organization and your plan does not include one. A locked section shows a lock and an **Upgrade plan** button. See [Organizations](/docs/teams/organizations).
